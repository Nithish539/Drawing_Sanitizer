Drawing layers

Every layer in the uploaded DXF, read in Step 1 and grouped the way rooms are: a layer whose name matches a family keyword joins that family, and the rest fall in behind the discipline letter they start with (A- architectural, S- structural, M- mechanical, and so on). Counts as wall is the one to read when a boundary comes out wrong: it says whether the boundary generator treats that layer's linework as a wall. Change that in walls in config.json.

Family rules

Which text goes under which boundary layer. Put LIFT LOBBY under Staircase and it will be drawn on AI_STAIR instead of AI_LIFT. One keyword per line, matched whole-word and case-insensitively.

Order decides ties. Families are checked top to bottom and the first match wins, so a keyword listed under two families belongs to the higher one. Longer keywords should sit above the shorter ones they contain (LIFT LOBBY above LIFT). Saving applies immediately — re-run Step 3 or Step 4 to see the effect.

Floor usages Every floor in Step 2 has a usage, read from its plan title by these words (else the default). A family limited to some usages below is used only on those floors; a family with none ticked is used on every floor.

Geometry (AI)

Everything found from the shape of the drawing, never from its texts: lifts, shafts, staircases, voids, toilets (WC pans and wash basins) - and a boundary for every enclosed room, named Room 1, Room 2 ... for you to give a family and a name. Select, trace, draw, merge or delete as needed, then Generate geometry boundaries for the geometry annotated DXF (the same AI_<FAMILY> layers, plus AI_COLUMN, AI_WINDOW, AI_P_DOORS / AI_S_DOORS and AI_WALL). The text-based Step 3 is not affected.

Built-in rules

These are the judgements made while drawing: that an X crossing marks a shaft, that a fill escaping its room should be distrusted, that a fire-hose tag is equipment and not a room. Untick one and it is not applied the next time you press Generate Boundaries in Step 3. Ticks are not saved: refreshing the page puts every rule back to its default. Hover a rule for what turning it off costs.
